# Scientific Limitations

- **Dataset Heterogeneity**: Integrating across different cell types (hNPC vs Huh7) captures conserved mechanisms but inherently loses tissue-specific nuance.
- **Lack of Experimental Validation**: Hub genes and repurposed drugs are computationally predicted; in vitro validation is required to confirm mechanistic efficacy.
- **Bystander Inference**: In bulk RNA-seq, the inability to distinguish infected from bystander cells dampens the true infection signature, which is why the scRNA-seq layer is critical.
